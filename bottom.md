What is Crusource?
Crusource is an HRMS and vendor management platform that helps businesses manage hiring demands from customers, fulfil them through recruiters and hiring vendors, and run day to day workforce operations for the people who get placed.
Is Crusource an HRMS or a vendor management system?
Both. Crusource combines core HRMS functions such as attendance, leave, timesheets and payslips with vendor and demand management functions such as customer tracking, contractor billing and hiring vendor coordination, in one platform.
Can Crusource manage both internal employees and external contractors?
Yes. Organisation Management covers your internal team, while Customer and Demand Management, along with Workforce Management, covers the contractors and employees placed with your customers.
Does Crusource include AI resume screening?
Yes. Crusource Screens lets recruiters upload resumes in bulk, match them against a job description, and get an instant shortlist, connected directly to your Talent Pool.
Can hiring vendors and BGV vendors access the platform directly?
Yes. Hiring vendors and background verification vendors are onboarded as users with their own scoped access, so demands and verification requests can be assigned and tracked without leaving the platform.
Is there a self service option for candidates?
Yes. The Candidate Portal lets applicants browse job openings, apply, track their application status and manage their own profile.
